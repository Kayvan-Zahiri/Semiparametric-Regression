Introduction

Selecting the right regression model can be tough. There’s different things to balance and take into account, like the domain, number of parameters to use and much more. Too many parameters could lead to overfilling whereas too few could result in the loss of important information. A solution to this is semiparametric regression. This approach uses a combination of parametric and nonparametric models. Parametric models have a finite set of parameters compared to nonparametric models which have infinite-dimensional parameters. Semiparametric models allow for a mix of both, providing more flexibility and opportunities for deeper analysis depending on the situation.

Key Insight:Semiparametric regression is the combination of finite-dimensional and infinite-dimensional parametric components, utilizing the strengths of both sides, incorporated into one.
